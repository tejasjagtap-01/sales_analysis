# 📊 Executive Retail Performance Dashboard

## 📝 Executive Summary
This project delivers a high-impact, single-page **Executive Command Center** built in Power BI Desktop to analyze multi-year retail transactions. Designed specifically for C-suite decision-makers, this report translates complex database schemas into interactive, non-technical insights covering financial health, structural product demand, and purchasing timelines.

---

## 🔑 Key Insights & Business Highlights

* **High-Margin Financials:** The storefront successfully commanded **$912K** in gross sales across 2,000 distinct transactions. With a Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) of **$189.76K**, the business maintains an exceptional gross profit margin of **79.19%** and a premium Average Order Value (AOV) of **$456**.
* **Perfect Inventory Equilibrium:** Operational risk is highly minimized across our product mix. Revenue streams split evenly across our three major pillars: **Electronics ($314K)**, **Clothing ($311K)**, and **Beauty ($287K)**.
* **Universal Target Audience:** Our active customer base exhibits a near-perfect split between **Female (51%)** and **Male (49%)** shoppers, proving that our product catalog carries powerful, gender-neutral mass appeal.
* **Compounding Momentum & Seasonal Spikes:** Timeline analysis reveals a steep upward growth curve shifting from 2022 to 2023. While daily baselines remain stable between a $20K–$40K corridor, the data exposes a massive post-holiday velocity spike on **December 26th**, generating **$7K** in a single 24-hour window.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Key Features
* **Data Visualizations:** Utilizes a highly efficient, scannable **4-Quadrant Z-Layout** to reduce cognitive load for non-technical users.
* **Dynamic Cross-Filtering:** Stakeholders can click any category, year, or gender to instantly filter the entire dashboard view.
